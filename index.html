Dim Sum Steamer Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026 · applies to the Dim Sum Steamer app for Android (package com.dimsumsteamer.game)

English中文

Dim Sum Steamer has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your progress stays on your phone. Purchases go through Google Play, and ads come from Google AdMob, which collects some device data as described below.

What the game keeps on your phone

The game saves your progress, stars, coins, boosters, recipe cards, treasures, your restaurant tower, settings and language choice in the app's own storage on your device. We do not receive or store this information. Uninstalling the app, clearing its storage in Android settings, or using "Reset progress" in the game deletes it.

The weekly leaderboard and Rush Hour rankings compare your scores with built-in teahouse chefs inside the app. Your scores are not uploaded or shared with other players.

Purchases

Coin packs, the starter pack, the Chef's Special offer, Remove Ads, the Lantern Pass gold track and the piggy bank are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles payment and your payment details; we never see them. The app receives a purchase token from Google Play so it can add what you bought and restore it later, and it uses that token only on your device. Google's handling of purchases is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: short reward videos that you choose to watch (for extra moves, a free steamer, double coins, wheel spins and similar rewards), and an occasional full-screen ad between levels. Ads never appear while you are playing a level, and the ads between levels stop for good once you buy Remove Ads, the starter pack or the Chef's Special. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects:

  • your device's IP address, which can indicate its approximate location;
  • how you interact with the ads and the app, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic information, such as app launch time and performance;
  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

This data is sent to Google over encrypted connections and handled under Google's policies. You can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ads are requested, and you can change your choice at any time with "Privacy choices" in the game's Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Reminders

If you agree to reminders, the app schedules notifications on your phone, for example when your red packet or daily reward is ready. Nothing about them is sent anywhere. You can turn them off in Android's notification settings.

Sharing

If you tap "Share" on a result screen, the game opens your phone's share menu with a short message. You choose where it goes; the game itself sends nothing.

Children

Dim Sum Steamer is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

《点心蒸笼》没有账号，也没有我们自己的服务器。你的游戏进度只保存在你的手机上。购买通过 Google Play 完成；广告由 Google AdMob 提供，它会按下文所述收集部分设备数据。

游戏在你手机上保存的内容

游戏会把你的进度、星星、金币、道具、食谱卡、珍宝、点心大楼、设置和语言选择保存在本应用自己的存储中。我们不会接收或保存这些信息。卸载应用、在安卓设置中清除应用存储，或在游戏中使用“重置进度”，都会将其删除。

每周排行榜和午市高峰排名是把你的分数与应用内置的茶楼师傅比较，你的分数不会上传，也不会与其他玩家共享。

购买

金币礼包、新手礼包、厨师特惠、去除广告、灯笼通行证金色奖励和小猪存钱罐通过 Google Play 结算系统出售。付款及付款信息由 Google 处理，我们无法看到。应用会从 Google Play 收到一个购买凭证，用于为你添加并在之后恢复所购内容，该凭证只在你的设备上使用。Google 对购买的处理适用 Google 隐私权政策。

广告（Google AdMob）

游戏会展示 Google AdMob 提供的广告：由你选择观看的奖励短视频（换取额外步数、免费蒸笼、双倍金币、转盘次数等奖励），以及在关卡之间偶尔出现的全屏广告。广告不会在关卡进行中出现；购买去除广告、新手礼包或厨师特惠之后，关卡之间的广告将永久停止。为了展示广告、衡量效果和防止欺诈，Google 移动广告 SDK 会收集：

  • 设备的 IP 地址（可推断大致位置）；
  • 你与广告和应用的互动，例如启动应用、点击和观看视频；
  • 诊断信息，例如应用启动时间和性能；
  • 设备标识符，例如安卓广告 ID 和应用集 ID。

这些数据通过加密连接发送给 Google，并按 Google 的政策处理。你可以阅读 Google 如何使用来自使用其服务的应用的信息。在欧洲经济区、英国和瑞士，应用会在请求任何广告之前通过 Google 的同意书征求你的同意，你也可以随时在游戏“设置”中的“隐私选项”更改选择。你还可以在手机设置中重置或删除广告 ID。

提醒

如果你同意接收提醒，应用会在你的手机上安排通知，例如红包或每日奖励可以领取时。相关信息不会发送到任何地方。你可以在安卓通知设置中关闭提醒。

分享

如果你在结果画面点击“分享”，游戏会打开手机的分享菜单并附上一段简短文字。分享到哪里由你决定，游戏本身不会发送任何内容。

儿童

《点心蒸笼》并非面向13岁以下儿童，我们不会在知情的情况下收集他们的个人信息。

政策变更
